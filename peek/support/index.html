Peek Support

Need help with Peek? Contact us at jake@onessa.app.

What to Include

To help us investigate, please include:

  • Your Peek app version
  • Your device model and iOS or macOS version
  • A short description of what happened and the steps that led to it
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain private schedule or task information

Calendars, Reminders, and Photos

Peek needs permission for the Apple data sources you choose to display. Open Apple Settings or System Settings, select Privacy & Security, and confirm that Peek has access to Calendar, Reminders, or Photos as needed. Then confirm the intended calendars and lists are selected inside Peek.

Widgets and Live Activities

On iPhone, confirm that Live Activities, notifications, and Background App Refresh are enabled for Peek. Open Peek after changing sources or settings so the latest selection can be prepared for widgets and Live Activities. System scheduling may delay background updates.

Purchases

The unified Peek app (App Store ID: 6758136586) uses the same Premium product on supported iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions. If a purchase of that shared product is not recognized, use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple handles billing and refunds.

Moving from the Standalone iPhone App

The former iPhone app (App Store ID: 6760283607) is no longer available to new users. Its purchase history is not automatically merged into the unified app.

The steps below apply once the 3.4.0 migration update and the unified iPhone app are available. Until then, keep the installed app and its data.

  1. Update the old iPhone app to the migration version, 3.4.0 or later, and open it.
  2. In Settings, choose Move to Peek for iPhone and Mac. This saves your settings and widget photo locally before opening the unified app listing.
  3. Install the unified app. If you have already opened it, close it completely and reopen it to import the local handoff. Settings you have already chosen in the new app are preserved.
  4. Grant Calendar, Reminders, or Photos permission again as needed. Re-add widgets and start any Live Activity from the unified app.
  5. Check your settings and access before deleting the old app.

Verified production Premium access from the old app can be recognized on the same iPhone after running the migration update. This local access does not create a purchase of the shared product and does not synchronize to another device or Mac. For access on another device, or if you already deleted the old app, contact jake@onessa.app with proof of the original purchase. Do not include payment-card details.

If no local handoff was prepared and the old app's local data has been deleted, the unified app cannot recover those settings or photos. Your source Calendar, Reminders, Photos, and Todoist data remain with their original services.
