Peek Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Onessa ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Peek on Apple platforms, including its widgets and Live Activities (collectively, the "App").

1. Privacy at a Glance

We do not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not operate an Onessa server that receives your calendar, reminder, photo, or task content. Most processing occurs on your device. If you enable an external task integration, the App communicates directly with that provider.

2. Apple Data and Permissions

  • Calendar and reminders: If you grant permission, Peek can read the calendars, events, reminder lists, and reminders you select. It can also create, update, complete, reopen, or delete items when you ask it to.
  • Photos and files: If you choose a photo or image file for a widget, Peek accesses that selected item and stores an app-sized copy locally. The favorite-photo feature may access photos you marked as favorites through Apple's Photos framework, including items stored in iCloud Photos.
  • Widgets and Live Activities: Peek stores a limited snapshot of selected schedule information and preferences in a local App Group container so its widget and Live Activity extensions can display it.

Apple processes Calendar, Reminders, Photos, App Store, and iCloud data under Apple's Privacy Policy. Peek does not request precise location.

3. Optional Task Integrations

  • Todoist: If enabled, Peek sends your API token and requests directly to Todoist to retrieve selected projects and tasks and to complete or reopen tasks. Todoist task details are displayed and temporarily cached on your device.
  • TickTick: If enabled where available, Peek sends your access token and requests directly to TickTick to retrieve selected projects and tasks and to complete or reopen tasks. TickTick task details are displayed and temporarily cached on your device.
  • Things: On macOS, if enabled, Peek reads the Things database folder that you explicitly select. A security-scoped bookmark and optional Things authorization token are stored locally. Completion actions are handed to the Things app.

Todoist and TickTick tokens are stored in the Apple Keychain on the device. Project selections, Things access information, and integration settings are stored locally. These services are governed by the respective provider's terms and privacy policy, and we do not control how they process your account data.

4. Other Local Data

Peek stores settings such as selected calendars, reminder lists, task projects, themes, display preferences, widget configuration, and review-request state locally. The App may also keep short-lived caches needed to display your timeline and refresh widgets or Live Activities.

5. Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time Premium purchase, receipt, and entitlement status. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

6. Information You Send to Us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and comply with applicable law. We do not sell or rent your information.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local settings, cached content, bookmarks, tokens, and selected widget images remain until you remove them, disable the relevant integration, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling Peek does not delete source data held by Apple Calendar, Reminders, Photos, Todoist, TickTick, or Things. You can revoke system permissions in Apple Settings and revoke or replace external-service tokens through the relevant provider.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

8. Security

Peek uses Apple platform protections such as app sandboxing, system permission controls, security-scoped bookmarks, App Groups, and Keychain storage where applicable. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Peek is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an Onessa-operated service. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

11. Contact Us
