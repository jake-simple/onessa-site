Peek Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Onessa ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Peek for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, including iOS widgets and Live Activities (collectively, the "App"). App Store ID: 6758136586.

1. Privacy at a Glance

We do not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not operate an Onessa server that receives your calendar, reminder, photo, or task content. Most processing occurs on your device. If you enable Todoist, the App communicates directly with Todoist.

2. Apple Data and Permissions

  • Calendar and reminders: If you grant permission, Peek can read the calendars, events, reminder lists, and reminders you select. It can also create, update, complete, reopen, or delete items when you ask it to.
  • Photos and files: If you choose a photo or image file for the App's header, Peek accesses that selected item and stores an app-sized copy locally. The favorite-photo feature may access photos you marked as favorites through Apple's Photos framework, including items stored in iCloud Photos.

Apple processes Calendar, Reminders, Photos, App Store, and iCloud data under Apple's Privacy Policy. Peek does not request precise location.

3. Optional Todoist Integration

If you enable Todoist, Peek sends your API token and requests directly to Todoist to retrieve selected projects and tasks and to complete or reopen tasks. Todoist task details are displayed and temporarily cached on your device. The token is stored in the Apple Keychain, while project selections and integration settings are stored locally. Todoist handles your account data under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Other Local Data

Peek stores settings such as selected calendars, reminder lists, Todoist projects, themes, display preferences, menu-bar configuration, integration settings, and review-request state locally. The App may also keep short-lived caches needed to display your timeline.

On iPhone and iPad, widgets and Live Activities use a limited local snapshot of selected schedules and preferences in an App Group container. If you use the migration update of the retired standalone iPhone app, it can also place settings and a selected widget photo in this shared local container for the unified app to import. Todoist credentials and a verified legacy purchase-access record are transferred through the shared Apple Keychain, not through the settings file. This handoff stays on the device and is not sent to an Onessa server.

5. Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time Premium purchase, receipt, and entitlement status. We do not receive your full payment-card details. The unified Peek app (App Store ID: 6758136586) uses one Premium product across its supported iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions. Purchases of this shared product can be restored with the Apple Account used to purchase it. The retired standalone iPhone app (App Store ID: 6760283607) used a separate product whose purchase history is not automatically merged. Its migration update can preserve verified legacy access on the same iPhone; that local access does not create a purchase of the shared product or synchronize to another device. See Peek Support for migration assistance.

6. Information You Send to Us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and comply with applicable law. We do not sell or rent your information.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local settings, cached content, tokens, and selected images remain until you remove them, disable the relevant feature, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling Peek does not delete source data held by Apple Calendar, Reminders, Photos, or Todoist. Shared App Group data may remain while another installed Peek app or extension uses the group, and Keychain items may remain after uninstalling an app. You can revoke system permissions in Settings or System Settings and revoke or replace your Todoist token through Todoist.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

8. Security

Peek uses Apple platform protections such as app sandboxing, system permission controls, security-scoped bookmarks, and Keychain storage where applicable. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Peek is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an Onessa-operated service. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

11. Contact Us
