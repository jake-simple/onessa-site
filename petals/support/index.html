Petals Support

Need help with Petals? Contact us at jake@onessa.app.

What to Include

To help us investigate, please include:

  • Your Petals app version
  • Your Mac model and macOS version
  • A short description of what happened and the steps that led to it
  • A screenshot, if it does not contain private calendar information

Calendar Access

Petals needs Calendar permission to show and edit events. Open System Settings, select Privacy & Security, then Calendars, and confirm that Petals is enabled. You can choose which calendars appear from within the App.

iCloud Sync

  • Confirm that you are signed in to the intended Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is enabled.
  • Make sure your Mac has an active internet connection.
  • Allow time for canvas items and imported images to download after signing in on a new Mac.

Imported Images

If an image does not appear, confirm that the source file was readable when imported and that iCloud Drive is available. Keep the original image until you have confirmed the import and synchronization completed successfully.
