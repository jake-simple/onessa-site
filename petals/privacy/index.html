Petals Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Onessa ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Petals (the "App").

1. Privacy at a Glance

We do not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not operate a separate account or analytics server for Petals. App content is stored on your Mac and, when iCloud is available, in your private iCloud, CloudKit, or iCloud Drive storage.

2. Information Handled by the App

  • Calendar data: If you grant permission, Petals can read calendars and events you select and can create, update, or delete events when you ask it to. Event details can include titles, dates, notes, locations, URLs, and calendar identifiers.
  • Petals content: Canvas items, vision-board content, text, layout information, themes, calendar sets, selected calendar identifiers, and other preferences you create or choose.
  • Images and files: Images you choose to import are copied into the App's local or iCloud Drive storage and may have thumbnails stored with your Petals content.
  • Technical settings: Local preferences and state needed to operate the App, such as the active calendar set and display choices.

Petals does not request access to Reminders or precise location. You can grant or revoke Calendar access in Apple Settings; disabling it makes calendar features unavailable.

3. Storage and iCloud Sync

Petals first attempts to store and sync its model data through Apple CloudKit and its images through iCloud Drive. If iCloud is unavailable, the App may use local storage instead. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not sell or rent your information.

4. Calendar Accounts

Petals accesses calendar data through Apple's EventKit framework. Calendars shown by Apple Calendar may be synchronized with an account provider you configured, such as iCloud, Google, or Exchange. Apple and that provider control their own synchronization and processing; Petals does not send calendar content to an Onessa server.

5. Information You Send to Us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and comply with applicable law.

6. Retention and Deletion

Your Petals content remains until you delete it through the App or the relevant Apple service. Uninstalling the App removes local app data but may not delete content stored in iCloud or calendar events saved to a calendar account. Because we do not keep a separate Onessa copy of your private Petals content, we generally cannot delete that content for you.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

7. Security

Petals uses Apple platform protections such as app sandboxing, security-scoped file access, system permission controls, and iCloud security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Petals is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an Onessa-operated service. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where applicable.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

10. Contact Us
