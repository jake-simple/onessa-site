Petals Privacy Policy

Petals ("the App") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

This app does not collect any personal information.

The app has the following characteristics:

  • Does not transmit data to any server
  • Does not use analytics tools
  • Does not display advertisements
  • Does not integrate with third-party services

2. Data Stored on Device

The app stores the following settings only on your device to improve user experience:

  • Selected calendars and reminder lists
  • Display settings (popover width, number of days to show, etc.)

This data is not transmitted outside your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. System Permissions

The app requests the following system permissions:

  • Calendar Access: Required to display calendar events
  • Reminders Access: Required to display reminder items

Data accessed through these permissions is managed by macOS. The app does not store this data separately or transmit it externally.

4. Data Security

All data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted over the network. The app runs in a macOS sandbox environment and cannot access data from other apps.

5. Children's Privacy

Since this app does not collect any personal information, it also does not collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, we will notify you through an app update.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy, please contact us at:

  • Email: simpleone.app@gmail.com

This privacy policy is effective as of January 22, 2026.
